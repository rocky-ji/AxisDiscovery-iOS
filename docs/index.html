Find devices on your local network.

Discover AXIS devices that advertise supported Bonjour services. View device names, IP addresses, and available serial numbers, then filter, sort, or copy details.

What it can find

Depending on their model and network configuration, compatible devices may include IP cameras, access control devices, network speakers, radar devices, and body-worn system controllers.

Designed for your local network

Discovery uses Bonjour/DNS-SD and, when available, the device's VAPIX Basic Device Information service. Scanning information is processed on your device and is not sent to the app developer.

Devices must advertise a supported Bonjour service and be reachable on the same local network. Guest Wi-Fi, VLANs, client isolation, or disabled Bonjour services may prevent discovery. The iOS app does not currently send SSDP or ONVIF multicast probes.
