Mac Privacy Policy

This policy describes Axis Device Discovery for Mac, an independent local network utility provided by Rocky Ji. It does not describe the separate iPhone/iPad app.

简体中文

Local device and server information

The app uses Bonjour/DNS-SD, SSDP, and ONVIF WS-Discovery. It processes advertised service names, hostnames, addresses, service metadata, and discovery responses to display device names, IP addresses, and available serial numbers. This may include IP addresses advertised by AXIS Camera Station servers; their serial numbers may be unavailable.

To fill in missing details, it may read a device's published UPnP description or make an anonymous VAPIX Basic Device Information request directly to that device. Device and firmware settings determine what information is available.

Scan results are held in memory for display. They are not uploaded to the developer or stored on a developer-controlled server. The app does not require an app account or device credentials, read video streams, access contacts, or change device settings.

Clipboard

When you choose Copy, the selected value is placed on the macOS clipboard. Other apps or system clipboard features may access copied content under their own behavior and settings. The app does not upload clipboard contents to the developer.

Network permission and security

Discovery and device requests require network access. Where macOS requests Local Network permission, you can grant or revoke it in system settings. Revoking permission may prevent discovery.

Depending on device configuration, local requests may use unencrypted HTTP. The current Mac implementation may accept self-signed HTTPS certificates from local devices for anonymous information requests. Use the app only on networks you trust and are authorized to administer. It is not a security or vulnerability assessment tool.

Payments, analytics, and tracking

The Mac app has no trial deadline, subscriptions, in-app purchases, advertising, or third-party analytics/tracking SDKs. It does not send scan results to a developer backend. Apple may separately process App Store or system diagnostic information under its own policies and your settings.

Support communications

If you email support, the developer receives your email address and any information you include. This is used to investigate and respond to your request. Please redact network identifiers where possible and never send passwords or camera footage. You may request deletion of support correspondence by emailing the address below, subject to applicable retention obligations.

This website

When published using GitHub Pages, this website is hosted by GitHub. GitHub may process website access information under its privacy statement. Website hosting is separate from the app's local discovery. These pages do not include analytics scripts or advertising.

Children and policy changes

The app is a network utility, not directed to children. This policy may be updated when functionality or data practices change; the date above identifies the current revision.

Mac 版隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 5 日。本政策适用于 Rocky Ji 提供的独立工具 Axis Device Discovery for Mac，不适用于单独的 iPhone/iPad 应用。

本地设备和服务器信息

应用使用 Bonjour/DNS-SD、SSDP 和 ONVIF WS-Discovery，处理服务名称、主机名、地址、服务元数据及发现响应，以显示名称、IP 地址和可读取的序列号。这可能包含 AXIS Camera Station 服务器广播的 IP，服务器序列号可能不可用。补全信息时可能直接读取设备公开的 UPnP 描述或发送匿名 VAPIX 请求。

扫描结果保存在运行内存中用于显示，不会上传给开发者或存入开发者控制的服务器。应用不要求 app 账号或设备密码，不读取视频流或通讯录，也不修改设备设置。

剪贴板

选择复制时，相应内容会写入 macOS 剪贴板。其他应用或系统剪贴板功能可能按其自身规则和设置访问这些内容；本应用不会向开发者上传剪贴板内容。

网络权限与安全

发现和设备请求需要网络访问。在提供“本地网络”权限提示的 macOS 版本中，可以在系统设置授予或撤销权限；撤销后发现功能可能不可用。本地请求可能使用未加密 HTTP，当前 Mac 实现还可能为匿名请求接受本地设备的自签名 HTTPS 证书。请仅在可信且获授权管理的网络中使用；本应用不是安全或漏洞评估工具。

付费、分析与追踪

Mac 版没有试用期限、订阅、应用内购买、广告或第三方分析/追踪 SDK，也不向开发者后端发送扫描结果。Apple 可能依照自身政策和你的设置处理 App Store 或系统诊断信息。

支持邮件

主动发送支持邮件时，开发者会收到你的邮箱地址及邮件中提供的信息，仅用于排查和回复。请遮盖敏感标识，不要发送密码或录像。可以通过下方邮箱申请删除支持邮件，但适用的法定留存义务除外。
