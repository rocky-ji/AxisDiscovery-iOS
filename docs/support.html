How can we help?

AXIS Device Discovery finds AXIS products that advertise supported Bonjour services on your local network.

No devices appear

  • Connect your iPhone or iPad and the AXIS device to the same Wi-Fi or local network.
  • Allow Local Network access when iOS asks. You can also check Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network.
  • Guest Wi-Fi, VLAN boundaries, firewall rules, and wireless client isolation can block Bonjour traffic.
  • The AXIS device must advertise one of the Bonjour services supported by the app. Devices that only respond to SSDP or ONVIF WS-Discovery may not appear in the iOS app.

Serial number is Unknown

Some devices do not publish a serial number over Bonjour. The app can only show a serial number when the device advertises it or makes it available through an anonymous local VAPIX request.

中文支持

没有发现设备

  • 请让 iPhone/iPad 与 AXIS 设备连接到同一 Wi-Fi 或局域网。
  • 首次提示时允许“本地网络”访问，也可在“设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 本地网络”中检查。
  • 访客 Wi-Fi、VLAN、网络防火墙或无线客户端隔离可能阻断 Bonjour 流量。
  • 设备需广播本 app 支持的 Bonjour 服务。只响应 SSDP 或 ONVIF WS-Discovery 的设备可能不会出现在 iOS 版中。

序列号显示“未知”

部分设备不会通过 Bonjour 广播序列号。只有设备公开广播该信息，或允许匿名本地 VAPIX 请求时，app 才能显示序列号。
