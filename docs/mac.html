Axis Device Discovery for Mac

Find compatible AXIS devices and AXIS Camera Station servers on your local network. View their advertised names, IP addresses, and available serial numbers in one searchable list.

Device discovery, without the clutter

  • Discover compatible devices using Bonjour/DNS-SD, SSDP, and ONVIF WS-Discovery.
  • Find supported IP cameras, intercoms, access controllers, network speakers, radar devices, and body-worn system controllers.
  • Find the IP addresses of AXIS Camera Station servers that respond to supported ONVIF WS-Discovery probes.
  • Filter names, IP addresses, and serial numbers with case-insensitive search.
  • Sort each column and select or copy device details.
  • Switch between English and Simplified Chinese and follow your Mac's light or dark appearance.

Known limitations

AXIS Camera Station: server IP discovery is supported when the server responds to the discovery protocol. Its serial number may be unavailable (NULL); the app may display this as Unknown. This is a known limitation and does not mean that IP discovery failed.

Discovery depends on the services enabled on each device and the network configuration. Guest Wi-Fi, wireless client isolation, VLAN boundaries, and firewalls may prevent discovery, even when a device's web page is accessible. Not every device or server can be discovered.

Free and local

The Mac app is free to use, with no trial deadline, subscription, or in-app purchases. The iPhone/iPad app is a separate product with its own purchase terms.

Scan results are processed locally and are not uploaded to the developer. The app reads advertised device information and may make anonymous local VAPIX requests to fill in missing details. No device credentials are required, and device settings are not changed.

The current minimum deployment target is macOS 12. Check your final App Store listing or distributed package for supported Mac architectures. A Mac App Store download link will be added when available.
