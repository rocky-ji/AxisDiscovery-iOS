Privacy Policy

AXIS Device Discovery is designed to find compatible AXIS devices on your local network. The app does not operate a developer-controlled account or cloud service.

Information processed by the app

When you scan, the app uses Bonjour/DNS-SD to discover supported local services. It may read a device's advertised name, IP address, and serial number. If available, it may send an anonymous VAPIX Basic Device Information request directly to that AXIS device on your local network to fill in missing device details.

Scan results are held in the app's memory for display. They are not transmitted to the app developer or stored on a developer-controlled server. The app does not request device credentials, access contacts, or change device settings.

Network and permissions

Local Network permission is required for Bonjour discovery and local device requests. You can revoke this permission in your device's system settings. Network discovery may not work on guest Wi-Fi, across VLANs, or where client isolation or firewall rules block local traffic.

Analytics, tracking, and sharing

The app does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking SDKs and does not share scan results with the developer or other companies. If you contact support by email, your message and email address are handled by your email provider and used only to respond.

Children's privacy

The app is a local network administration utility and is not directed to children.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the app's functionality or data practices change. For privacy questions, contact haichao_ji@163.com.

隐私政策

最后更新：2026 年 11 月 3 日

AXIS Device Discovery 用于发现局域网内兼容的 AXIS 设备。本 app 不运营开发者账号或云服务。

本 app 处理的信息

扫描时，app 使用 Bonjour/DNS-SD 发现支持的本地服务，并读取设备广播的名称、IP 地址和序列号。在设备支持且信息缺失时，app 可能直接向局域网内的 AXIS 设备发送匿名 VAPIX Basic Device Information 请求，以补全设备信息。

扫描结果仅保存在 app 运行内存中用于显示，不会发送给开发者或存入开发者控制的服务器。app 不要求设备账号密码、不访问通讯录，也不会修改设备设置。

网络权限

Bonjour 发现和本地设备请求需要“本地网络”权限。你可以在系统设置中撤销此权限。访客 Wi-Fi、VLAN、客户端隔离或防火墙规则可能阻止局域网发现。

分析、追踪与共享

app 不包含广告、分析或第三方追踪 SDK，也不会向开发者或其他公司分享扫描结果。若你通过邮件联系支持，邮件地址和内容由邮件服务提供商处理，仅用于回复你的请求。

儿童隐私

本 app 是局域网设备管理工具，不面向儿童。

政策变更与联系

若 app 功能或数据处理方式发生变化，本政策可能随之更新。隐私问题请联系：haichao_ji@163.com。
